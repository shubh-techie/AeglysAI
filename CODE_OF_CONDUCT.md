# AeglysAI community code of conduct

Treat contributors and participants respectfully, regardless of experience,
identity, background or programming language. Give constructive feedback on work.
Credit others and respect their licenses and privacy.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, unwanted contact, impersonation and
publication of another person's private information are unacceptable. Do not use
real credentials, target systems without authorization or pressure anyone to share
confidential work.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate public content, request a correction, pause
participation or restrict access. They should explain decisions privately where
possible, consider context and avoid disclosing reporter information. A maintainer
involved in a report should not decide it alone; the owner should arrange another
reviewer when available. Decisions may be reconsidered when new evidence arrives.

No dedicated confidential moderation channel is established. Request a private
channel from the owner without posting incident details publicly. Do not publish
names, screenshots or accusations containing private information. Immediate physical
safety concerns belong with appropriate local services, not a repository issue.
This policy applies to project-managed repository discussions and event spaces;
maintainer staffing and response times are not guaranteed.
