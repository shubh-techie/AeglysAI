# AeglysAI team administration contract

Team size: 1–4 participants. Individual participation is permitted and is a one-member team. Require exactly one team lead, including for an individual entry. Every member, including the lead, must be individually qualified and selected before competing. A lead's evidence cannot qualify another member.

Organizers assign a unique team ID and privately match every member to an approved registration ID. Check no duplicate active team membership across the event before approval and after any change. Team IDs are administrative identifiers; publish a separate approved showcase identifier when needed, without participant mappings.

## Team-change deadline and workflow

The organizer must publish an exact team-change cutoff with timezone before registration opens; its actual date is PENDING CONFIGURATION. Requests require the team ID, requested membership/lead change and affected members' agreement through the private official submission channel. An organizer checks individual qualification and selection, size 1–4, exactly one lead, approved registration matches and duplicate active membership before approval. Record the prior roster, new roster, reviewer, timestamp and reason privately; notify affected members and update Sheets atomically as an administrative procedure. This is not implemented software enforcement.

Changes after the cutoff require a documented organizer exception for an exceptional circumstance, equivalent treatment and renewed eligibility checks. Freeze an approved roster at submission and recheck before finalist demos. A departing member's identity must not remain in public materials without valid publication consent. No private roster, registration ID or participant personal information goes into Git or Portal.
