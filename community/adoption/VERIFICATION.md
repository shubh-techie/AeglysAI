# Verification and correction procedure

1. Obtain an owner-approved public artifact and permission to cite it; keep private
   correspondence and identifying material outside Git.
2. Verify source ownership, exact commit, public artifact bytes/digest, environment,
   integration or reproduction instructions and actually executed results.
3. Determine maintainer/external/unknown classification and independence/affiliation;
   document the method without exporting personal data.
4. Review limitations and supporting references. A screenshot, star or unsupported
   statement alone is insufficient. Ask for missing evidence privately.
5. A reviewer records draft or verified using the same status vocabulary as the
   underlying evidence standard. Verified independent adoption/reproduction requires
   external classification, independent relationship and an execution reference.
6. Corrections reference a superseded prior record, explain the reason and undergo
   a new review. Duplicate IDs, branching replacements and cycles are invalid.
7. Withdrawal sets record_state to withdrawn and verification to draft; exclude it
   from current impact summaries. Preserve only approved, minimized provenance.

The validator checks assertions and relationships offline. It does not contact
external services, verify signatures, confirm affiliations or certify real outcomes.
A verified fixture is synthetic, not a claim about any actual organization.
Consent, archival retention and public-reference approval require human decisions.
